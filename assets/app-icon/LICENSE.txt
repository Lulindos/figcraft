LegatIA Design app icon (figcraft.svg, figcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in
this directory: figcraft-<size>.png, figcraft.ico, hicolor/). The file names are the upstream ones
so that the build, the packaging scripts and `cargo xtask icon` keep working.

The mark is the Legat.IA brand mark ("IA" on a black rounded square), (c) Legat.IA, all rights
reserved. It is NOT covered by the Apache-2.0 / MIT licence of the rest of this repository: do not
reuse it outside LegatIA Design.

The upstream FigCraft icon (a chameleon, MIT OR Apache-2.0, by the FigCraft project owner) that
these files replace is at https://github.com/storytold/figcraft/tree/main/assets/app-icon.

`cargo xtask icon` rasterises figcraft.svg with resvg and regenerates the PNGs and the .ico.
